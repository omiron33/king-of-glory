The Sabbath held its breath above the tomb.
Below, a light disturbed the dead.
Satan came to Hades: “He died upon the cross.
The Nazarene is yours. Prepare His cell.”
But Hades shook: “He called four-day Lazarus;
My prisoner flew out at a single word.
If Jesus enters, every cell will open.
Do not bring Him here. We'll lose them all!”
Satan laughed; the chambers shook beneath him.
The captives turned toward the growing light.
Adam called to Seth: “Tell of mercy's oil.”
“The Son will come and heal my father's wound.”
Isaiah cried, “I spoke of light in darkness!”
John said, “I pointed to the Lamb of God.
At Jordan I saw the Spirit rest on Him;
I heard the Father call Him His own Son.”
David cried, “I sang that brass would break!”
Jeremiah said, “God would walk among us.”
The saints broke into praise. Now Satan shook;
He searched in vain for any way to flee.
Who is this King of Glory?
The Lord, the mighty One!
Lift up your gates for the King of Glory
Let the iron fall before His light
Jesus Christ, the deathless Lord
Has entered death and broken night
By His cross the chains are shattered
By His life the dead arise
Son of Mary, Son of God
The King of Glory reigns on high
Hades cried, “Bar every gate of brass!
Set every bolt across the iron doors!”
A thief arrived, his cross upon his back:
“I died beside Him. He heard my prayer.
He promised me His paradise today;
I come before Him. He is close behind.”
David cried, “Open to the King!”
The saints rose up; the thunder rolled again.
Who is this King of Glory?
The Lord, the mighty One!
Lift up your gates for the King of Glory
Let the iron fall before His light
Jesus Christ, the deathless Lord
Has entered death and broken night
The gates of brass burst outward; iron fell.
The King came through in human form and glory.
The deepest prison flooded with His light;
Satan staggered backward, seeking flight.
Christ caught and bound the father of the lie;
He gave him over to the keeper of the dead:
“Hold him until the day I come again.”
The jailer held the one who filled his cells.
Jesus crossed the ruins, calling Adam:
“Peace to you and all your children.”
He raised him; Adam knew the hands that made him.
Eve fell weeping at her Lord's feet;
He raised her; she knew those hands as well.
One tree had led them into death;
Christ's cross became their road to life.
He led the righteous through the broken gates.
They asked Him for a sign of victory;
He set His cross where death had held its throne.
The thief had carried his across the threshold;
Now Adam's children followed Christ toward home.
Lift up your gates for the King of Glory
Let the iron fall before His light
Jesus Christ, the deathless Lord
Has entered death and broken night
By His cross the chains are shattered
By His life the dead arise
Son of Mary, Son of God
The King of Glory reigns on high
Christ is risen. Adam rises.
Eve comes walking into day.
The King of Glory leads His people
Out of death and into life.

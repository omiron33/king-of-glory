# Storyboard: King of Glory (The Gates of Hades)

Written before any animation. One row per stretch of the song. Read the lyric column top to bottom with the sound off: the story should still come through. The last column says how the words are part of that world. The picture follows the Orthodox icon of the Resurrection (the Anastasis): Christ in a mandorla of light standing on the broken gates, Satan bound, Adam and Eve raised by the wrist, the righteous behind. No faces: people are silhouettes, hands and light; Christ is light, vestments and the mandorla.
